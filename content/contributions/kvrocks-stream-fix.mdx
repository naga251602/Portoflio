## The problem

Repeated stream entry IDs could be double-counted during deletion, allowing an unsigned stream length to underflow.

## The fix

Deduplicate IDs per deletion request, guard the length calculation and test repeated IDs.

[Read the technical write-up](/blog/kvrocks-xdel-duplicate-ids) and [see the pull request](https://github.com/apache/kvrocks/pull/3701).
