## Research question

How well does DenseNet detect pneumonia in chest X-rays compared with other common CNNs?

## Method

Co-trained DenseNet-121 on the CheXpert chest X-ray dataset with augmentation and class weighting to handle label imbalance, and benchmarked it against ResNet-50 and VGG-16.

## Findings

Reached 92% precision-recall AUC on the held-out test set. DenseNet beat the other models by 6% AUC, thanks to dense skip connections that let layers reuse features.
