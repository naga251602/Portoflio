## Research question

How can GPT-based pipelines generate and personalize website copy from keywords, and how does it compare with human-written copy?

## Method

Co-developed the keyword-driven recommendation engine, then compared its output with human-written baselines in user studies with 40+ participants who scored coherence, relevance and preference.

## Findings

Reduced manual authoring time by 60%. The paper won Best Paper, 3rd Place at the 2024 International Conference on Computing and Data Science (ICCDS).
