## Research question

Can the sentiment in patient reviews be used to rank drug recommendations?

## Method

Co-developed an NLP pipeline with a fine-tuned BERT model on domain-specific patient review data, and compared it against TF-IDF and LSTM baselines.

## Findings

Reached 88% classification accuracy, with a 12-point F1 improvement over the baselines from transfer learning with biomedical pretraining.
