- Built an iOS app on AWS Serverless that lets users bookmark restaurants by forwarding a WhatsApp text, IG reel, FB message, or email, so there is no manual entry.
- Collapsed **5+ scattered apps into 1** by building maps, reminders, and directions natively in Swift, with OAuth/OTP sign-in.
- Used gRPC streaming between the Swift app and the backend to push share-ingestion status and saved-restaurant sync in **under 100ms**, replacing REST polling that took 2 to 3s per refresh.
