- Cut response time to deals from hours to **under 60 seconds** with a Cheerio scraping pipeline that parses live prices and sends transactional emails through Nodemailer when a target price is hit.
- Reduced redundant scrape requests **~40%** across 200+ tracked products with cross-user URL deduplication on hourly cron checks.
- Secured the app with Supabase Auth (email/password and Google OAuth) and Row Level Security policies, plus a token-based one-click unsubscribe that needs no login.

Live demo: [pricewatch-eight-rosy.vercel.app](https://pricewatch-eight-rosy.vercel.app/)
