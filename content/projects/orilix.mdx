- Cut average patient intake time **45%** (12 min to 6.5 min) by replacing static forms with a Gemini-driven adaptive questionnaire that asks about 14 contextual questions instead of 40 fixed ones.
- Generated structured SOAP notes from intake responses in **under 10 seconds** vs. about 15 minutes manually, and transcribed doctor dictation into SOAP, summary, and bullet formats.
- Extracted vitals from free-text dictation into structured JSON with **95% accuracy**, with Firebase Auth (JWT) securing clinic staff access and Firestore storing patient records.
